Understanding Diabetic Retinopathy Stages

The International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) scale defines 5 distinct stages of disease progression.

STAGE 0

Normal / Healthy Retina

Clear blood vessels, healthy optic disc, and clean macula. Zero microaneurysms or bleeding.

Action: Annual checkup at local PHC.
STAGE 1

Mild Non-Proliferative (NPDR)

Earliest stage: tiny balloon-like swelling in retinal blood vessels (microaneurysms).

Action: 6–12 month screening & BP check.
STAGE 2

Moderate Non-Proliferative

Blood vessels swell, leak blood (blot hemorrhages), and deposit yellow lipids (hard exudates).

Action: District eye specialist (6–8 wks).
STAGE 4

Proliferative Retinopathy (PDR)

Advanced stage: fragile abnormal new blood vessels grow and bleed into the eye. High risk of blindness.

Action: URGENT tertiary care (< 2 wks).
